About

One of Devil Jin's black feathers drifts down, and I'm back in Bangladesh playing Tekken 5 on the PS2. I remember wondering if Heihachi was really dead, but the part of the opening that stuck with me most was Asuka riding her bike across the rooftops. I wanted to pick her before I even knew how she fought. Before I got into DC Comics and superheroes, I had Tekken.

Tekken 5 is my second-favorite game of all time, right behind The Last of Us. I miss how serious it felt, from the mystery around the Mishima family to the way Devil Jin looked in black leather and chains. I still put "Sparking!" on Spotify when I'm driving or studying. And judging by the chrome lettering and chains I picked for this page, the look of that game stuck with me just as much as the music did.

The Story

So after Tekken 4, Heihachi gets jumped by a bunch of Jack-4 robots in the Hon-Maru temple. Huge explosion. Everyone thinks hes dead. Kazuya gets thrown off a cliff again (this family is insane) and then Jinpachi Mishima breaks free from under the temple. Hes Heihachis father and hes been locked up for decades.

Jinpachi got possessed by some demonic force and hes losing control so he starts the King of Iron Fist Tournament 5 basically begging someone to come and stop him before he destroys everything. Jin Kazama enters to try and end the Mishima bloodline curse for good.

"I am Jinpachi Mishima. I need the help of a strong soul. If you are reading this, I am probably already a monster."

My Mains

32 characters in this game. Here are the ones I rock with:

Jin Kazama Dropped the Mishima style for traditional karate. Hes cold, hes angry, and hes here to end the bloodline.
Devil Jin Jin but demonic. Laser beams, wings, insane combos. High risk high reward.
Hwoarang Tae Kwon Do kicks for days. Jins rival. Nonstop pressure and way too much swag.
Asuka Kazama Jins cousin, new to Tekken 5. Aikido reversals that punish you for being too aggressive.
Raven Ninja agent. Teleports, shurikens, one of the hardest character designs in any fighting game.
Feng Wei Chinese kenpo fighter. Came to steal ancient scrolls. Hits like a truck.

Gameplay

They got rid of the weird uneven floors from Tekken 4 and brought back flat stages. The 4-button system stayed the same — left punch, right punch, left kick, right kick — but combos got way deeper.

What Made It Great

  1. Crush system — highs whiff on crouching, lows get crushed by jumps. Huge for mindgames.
  2. 32 characters — each with 100+ moves. Nobody plays the same.
  3. Arcade History — Tekken 1, 2, and 3 fully playable on the disc. Insane value.
  4. Devil Within — beat-em-up side mode with Jin in the Mishima labs.
  5. Customization — costumes, accessories, colors for every fighter.

Soundtrack

One of the hardest OSTs in gaming. Electronic, rock, industrial, orchestral — every stage has a banger.

Best Tracks

  • "Moonlit Wilderness" — THE Tekken 5 track. Haunting and goes stupid hard.
  • "Sparking" — the opening theme. Pure adrenaline.
  • "Ground Zero Funk" — Burning Temple stage. Groovy but aggressive.
  • "Formless Like Water" — chill and hypnotic.
  • "Crimson Sunset" — final stage theme. Chills every time.

Why Its the GOAT

This is the game that got me into fighting games. The way every hit has weight to it, the character depth, the style of the menus and cutscenes — nothing else comes close for me.

Single player alone is crazy. Story Mode, Arcade History, Devil Within, customization grinding — you could play for hundreds of hours and never touch VS mode.

And the whole aesthetic of this game is just unmatched. Dark, metallic, 2000s gothic energy. Chrome menus, brooding character designs. It looks like it was forged in a factory and set on fire.
